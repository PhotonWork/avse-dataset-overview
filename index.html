02 / REAL2MIX RECORDING PROTOCOL

Three stages. One shared scene.

Solo recordings provide identity references. Jointly recorded overlap provides the evaluation mixture.

01

Speaker A only

Scene-matched reference speech for target A.

02

Speaker B only

Scene-matched reference speech for target B.

03

A + B together

Both speakers share the room, reverberation, and background sound.

Geometry & views

  • Recording distances: 0.5, 1.5, and 3 m.
  • Side-camera placement angles: 30°, 45°, and 60°.
  • Three synchronized views capture the same two-speaker event.
  • Benchmark evaluation uses predominantly frontal views.

Camera placement angles are not measured acoustic target directions.

From recordings to queries

Full recordings are manually segmented, aiming to preserve complete utterances. Clips retain a consistent mapping between the two visual tracks and speaker labels.

6,038 mixtures × 2 targets = 12,076 queries

The collection contains 1,556 solo and 6,042 overlap clips; four overlap clips lack a complete ROI pair, leaving 6,038 eligible mixtures. Solo references are different utterances, not synchronized clean sources.

03 / VOXBLINK2-AVSE CURATION

Aligned pairs, separated identities.

Validate decodable audio and synchronized mouth tracks, then retain pairs meeting the predicted speech-quality threshold. Rejected pairs are discarded rather than enhanced.
